## 1. Business Structure Options

| Structure | Best For | Liability | Tax | Admin Burden |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sole Proprietorship** | Solo services, freelance | Unlimited personal liability | Personal income tax | Lowest |
| **Partnership** | 2–4 founders, services | Shared personal liability | Partners taxed individually | Low |
| **LLC / Limited Company** | Most startups and SMEs | Limited to capital contributed | Corporate tax | Medium |
| **Joint Stock Company (JSC)** | Scaling businesses, investors | Limited | Corporate tax | Higher |
| **Foreign-Owned Enterprise** | Foreign investors | Limited | Corporate tax + restrictions | Highest |

**Recommendation for most early-stage startups**: LLC / Limited Liability Company (Công ty TNHH in Vietnam). Balances protection, flexibility, and tax efficiency.

## 2. Vietnam Legal Framework

### Company Registration
- Governing body: Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI) / Department of Planning and Investment (DKI)
- Process: Register via National Business Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn)
- Timeline: 3–5 working days for basic registration
- Required: Charter capital declaration, founder identification, registered address
- Charter capital: No minimum for most sectors; some sectors have minimums (banking, insurance, securities)

### Business License (Giấy phép kinh doanh)
- Required for regulated sectors: food, pharma, education, finance, healthcare, logistics
- Issued by sector ministry or provincial authority
- Timeline: 15–60 days depending on sector
- Always check: Does your VSIC business code require a sector license?

### Tax Registration
- VAT: Register if revenue >VND 1 billion/year (mandatory) or voluntarily
- Corporate Income Tax (CIT): Standard rate 20%; reduced rates available for high-tech, agriculture
- Personal Income Tax (PIT): Employer must register for payroll withholding
- Social Insurance: Mandatory for employees (employer contributes ~17.5%, employee ~10.5%)

### Employment Law Key Points
- Labor Code 2019 governs all employment relationships
- Probationary period: max 60 days (30 days for roles below professional level)
- Termination: Requires cause and proper notice (30–45 days); severance rules apply
- Written employment contracts required for roles >1 month
- Non-compete clauses: Enforceable but limited in scope and duration

## 3. Regional Legal Context (SEA)

### Singapore
- Ease of business: Top 2 globally
- Structure: Pte Ltd (Private Limited) most common
- Register via: BizFile+ / ACRA
- GST: 9% (2024+); registration mandatory if turnover >SGD 1M
- Employment Pass for foreign founders: Requires salary min SGD 5,000/month

### Thailand
- Foreign Business Act: Restricts foreign ownership in many sectors (51% Thai ownership required in restricted sectors)
- BOI promotion available for qualifying businesses (tax holidays, land ownership rights)
- Key licenses: FDA (food/health), FBA license, Revenue Department registration

### Indonesia
- Omnibus Law (2020) simplified foreign investment but sector restrictions remain
- PT PMA (foreign-owned company) requires minimum investment IDR 10 billion
- OSS (Online Single Submission) system for business registration
- Halal certification required for food/beverage businesses

### Malaysia
- Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) registration
- Sdn Bhd (private limited) standard structure
- MyIPO for trademark and patent registration
- SST (Sales and Service Tax): 6–10% depending on category

## 4. IP Protection

### Trademarks
- Register in every country where you operate or plan to operate
- Vietnam: Register via National Office of Intellectual Property (NOIP / IP Vietnam)
- International: Madrid Protocol covers 130+ countries via a single application
- Timeline: 12–18 months for Vietnam; similar in most SEA countries
- Cost: ~$200–$500 per class per country
- **Do this early** — trademark disputes are expensive and can kill a brand

### Patents
- Relevant for: hardware, software with novel algorithms, unique manufacturing processes
- Vietnam: NOIP handles patent applications
- Timeline: 18–36 months; costs $500–$5,000+
- Consider: Is the innovation truly novel? Would trade secret protection be better?

### Copyright
- Automatic upon creation in Vietnam and most countries (no registration required)
- Register for evidentiary advantage: Vietnam Copyright Office
- Covers: code, content, design, creative works
- Does NOT protect: business ideas, names, or methods

### Trade Secrets
- Protect via: NDAs, non-disclosure provisions in employment contracts, access controls
- Cheaper and faster than patents but requires active protection effort
- Best for: processes, formulas, customer lists, algorithms

## 5. Data Privacy Compliance

### Vietnam — Decree 13/2023/ND-CP (Personal Data Protection)
- Effective: July 2023
- Applies to: Any organization processing personal data of Vietnamese citizens
- Key requirements:
  - Obtain explicit consent before collecting personal data
  - Define and disclose purpose of data processing
  - Provide data access, correction, and deletion rights
  - Appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO) for large-scale processors
  - Report data breaches to Ministry of Public Security within 72 hours
  - Cross-border data transfers require government approval in sensitive categories

### GDPR (EU — if serving EU customers)
- Applies regardless of where your company is registered
- Heavy penalties: up to 4% of global annual turnover
- Key: lawful basis for processing, data minimization, breach notification

### PDPA (Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia variants)
- Similar structure to GDPR but jurisdiction-specific
- Singapore PDPA: Mandatory data breach notification within 3 days

### HIPAA (US — if handling US healthcare data)
- Strict technical and administrative safeguards required
- Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) required with all vendors touching PHI

## 6. Sector-Specific Regulations by Industry Subagent

### Fintech Strategist
- Vietnam: SBV License required for payment intermediaries (Circular 09/2020)
- P2P lending: No formal license yet but SBV regulatory sandbox available
- Crypto/digital assets: Currently restricted; watch for regulatory updates
- Anti-money laundering (AML): Required policies for all fintech handling transactions

### EdTech Growth Expert
- Vietnam: Educational service businesses require licensing from MOET or provincial DoET
- Foreign-language centers: Specific permit required (Nghị định 46/2017)
- Online education platforms (Thông tư 09/2021): Must comply for platform-based learning
- Data of minors: Stricter consent requirements under Decree 13/2023

### D2C Commerce Specialist
- Vietnam e-commerce: Register on Ministry of Industry and Trade portal (online.gov.vn)
- Consumer Protection Law: Returns, refunds, product liability
- Customs and import duties: For cross-border goods
- Counterfeit goods law: Strict enforcement — ensure supplier authenticity

### F&B Operations Expert
- Vietnam: Food Safety Certificate required (Cục An toàn thực phẩm / local health authority)
- Fire safety certificate: Required for all food establishments
- Business license + sector license + fire safety = 3 key permits before opening
- HACCP certification: Required for food manufacturing/export

### Healthcare / MedTech Expert
- Medical device registration: Vietnam FDA (DAV) — Class A–D based on risk
- Timeline: 6 months (Class A) to 3+ years (Class D)
- Pharmaceutical: MOH approval required; complex and lengthy
- Telemedicine: Circular 49/2017 governs; requires medical examination facility license

### Real Estate Strategist
- Vietnam: Real estate business license + legal capital VND 20 billion minimum
- Foreign ownership: Only for residential; limited to 30% of units per project
- Project escrow requirement: 5% of project value held in escrow

## 7. Legal Checklist by Stage

### Pre-Launch
- [ ] Company registered with correct business code (VSIC)
- [ ] Bank account opened in company name
- [ ] Tax registration completed (MST issued)
- [ ] Founder agreements / shareholder agreement signed
- [ ] IP filings initiated (trademark at minimum)
- [ ] Employment contracts drafted for first hires
- [ ] Terms of Service and Privacy Policy published (if digital product)

### Launch
- [ ] Sector-specific licenses obtained (if required)
- [ ] Social insurance registration for employees
- [ ] Accounting system set up (VAT invoicing compliant)
- [ ] Data processing agreements with key vendors signed
- [ ] Fire safety and workplace safety compliance verified

### Growth (6–12 months)
- [ ] Trademark registered (not just applied)
- [ ] Employment handbook / HR policies documented
- [ ] Annual compliance filings on schedule
- [ ] Review foreign ownership rules if seeking international investment
- [ ] Review data privacy compliance as user data grows

> ⚠️ *Always consult a licensed lawyer in your jurisdiction. Laws change — verify current status before acting.*
