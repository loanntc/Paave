## 1. Market Sizing Framework

### TAM / SAM / SOM

```
TAM (Total Addressable Market)
└── Everyone globally who could ever buy this
    └── SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
        └── The portion you can realistically reach (geography, language, channel)
            └── SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
                └── Realistic capture in Year 1–3 given budget and competition
```

**Top-Down Method**: Industry report total → apply segment % → apply geography %
**Bottom-Up Method**: Count potential customers × average spend per year = market size
**Value-Theory Method**: What would customers pay × how many exist = value-based TAM

*Best practice: use all three and triangulate. If they diverge by >2x, dig deeper.*

### SOM Reality Check
- SOM should be ≤5% of SAM for a new entrant in Year 1
- For niche markets, SOM can be higher (10–20%) if distribution is focused
- Always ask: "Could we operationally serve this many customers in Year 1?"

## 2. Primary Research Methods

### Customer Interviews (Highest signal)
- **Target**: 10–20 interviews with your ideal customer profile
- **Goal**: Understand pain, current behavior, willingness to pay
- **Key questions**:
  - "Walk me through the last time you faced [problem]"
  - "What do you use today to solve it?"
  - "What's most frustrating about current solutions?"
  - "If a perfect solution existed, what would you pay?"
  - "Who else in your organization is involved in this decision?"
- **Red flags**: You're pitching, not listening. Stop if you're talking >30% of the time.

### Surveys
- Use: Google Forms, Typeform, Tally
- Sample size: 50+ for directional signal, 200+ for statistical confidence
- Best for: Quantifying what interviews reveal qualitatively
- Avoid: Leading questions, binary yes/no on willingness to pay (use price ranges instead)

### Landing Page Smoke Test
- Build a simple page describing the product (no product built yet)
- Drive traffic via small paid ads ($100–$300) or organic social
- Measure: email signups, waitlist joins, click-throughs on "Buy Now"
- Conversion rate >2% = strong signal; >5% = exceptional

### Competitor Customer Mining
- Read 1-star AND 5-star reviews on competitors (G2, Trustpilot, App Store, Shopee, etc.)
- 1-star reviews = unmet needs = your opportunity
- 5-star reviews = what customers value most = match or exceed this
- Mine forums: Reddit, Facebook Groups, local community boards

## 3. Secondary Research Sources by Industry

### Universal Sources
| Source | What You'll Find |
|--------|----------------|
| Google Trends | Demand trajectory, seasonality, geographic demand |
| Statista / IBISWorld | Market size, industry reports, benchmarks |
| SimilarWeb | Competitor web traffic, channel breakdown |
| LinkedIn | Company sizes, hiring trends, decision-maker profiles |
| Reddit / Facebook Groups | Raw customer language and pain points |
| App Store / Play Store reviews | Product feedback on competitors |
| Government statistics portals | Demographics, economic data, trade data |
| Trade associations | Industry benchmarks and regulatory updates |
| Job postings | What companies are investing in = business priorities |

### Industry-Specific Sources

**Fintech / Payments**
- Central bank reports (SBV Vietnam, BNM Malaysia, MAS Singapore)
- Fintech reports: CB Insights Fintech, KPMG Pulse of Fintech
- Nilson Report (payments industry)

**EdTech**
- Ministry of Education statistics (national)
- HolonIQ EdTech intelligence reports
- Class Central (online course market data)

**E-commerce / Retail**
- eMarketer / Insider Intelligence
- Shopify Commerce Trends report
- Lazada/Shopee seller data and category reports

**SaaS / B2B Software**
- OpenView Partners SaaS benchmarks
- Bessemer Cloud Index
- G2 Grid Reports by category

**F&B / Restaurant**
- National Restaurant Association reports
- Euromonitor (foodservice)
- Local food safety authority statistics

**Healthcare / MedTech**
- WHO global health statistics
- EvaluateMedTech / EvaluatePharma
- ClinicalTrials.gov (competitive pipeline)

**Real Estate / PropTech**
- CBRE / JLL market reports
- PropertyGuru data (Southeast Asia)
- Land registry statistics (national)

## 4. Customer Persona Template

```
Name:               [Fictional archetype name]
Age / Demographics: 
Job / Role:         
Goals:              What are they trying to achieve?
Frustrations:       What gets in the way today?
Current solutions:  What do they use right now?
Decision triggers:  What would make them switch or buy?
Decision factors:   Price? Speed? Trust? Brand? Features?
Where they spend time: Online communities, platforms, events
Willingness to pay: $X–$Y per [month/unit/year]
Quote:              A real or archetypal quote capturing their mindset
```

Build 2–3 personas minimum. Rank them as Primary, Secondary, Tertiary.

## 5. Demand Validation Checklist

- [ ] Can you name 10 real people who would pay for this today?
- [ ] Have you interviewed at least 5 potential customers?
- [ ] Is there evidence people search for this problem online (Google Trends > 0)?
- [ ] Are there competitors making money in this space? (Good sign — market exists)
- [ ] Has anyone pre-paid, signed up, or expressed strong purchase intent?
- [ ] Do customers describe the problem in the same words without prompting?
- [ ] Is the TAM large enough to support your revenue goals (TAM × 1% > target)?
- [ ] Can you reach your ICP through an affordable channel?

## 6. Market Trend Analysis Framework

Analyze signals across 4 dimensions:

| Dimension | What to Look For |
|---|---|
| **Macro trends** | Demographics, economic shifts, urbanization, income growth |
| **Technology trends** | Enabling technologies (mobile, AI, cloud) that make this possible now |
| **Cultural trends** | Changing values, behaviors, generational shifts |
| **Regulatory trends** | Laws changing that create or close opportunities |

**Timing signal**: Why is now the right time? What changed in the last 2–3 years that wasn't true before?

Tools: Google Trends, Exploding Topics, SparkToro, LinkedIn Insights, CB Insights

## 7. Research Toolkit by Industry Subagent

### Fintech Strategist
- Primary: Interview CFOs, SME owners, retail banking customers
- Secondary: SBV reports, CB Insights fintech data, Crunchbase funding rounds
- Validation: Check competitor app store ratings and review sentiment

### EdTech Growth Expert  
- Primary: Interview teachers, principals, parents, students (separate interviews per persona)
- Secondary: Ministry of Education enrollment data, HolonIQ, UNESCO Institute for Statistics
- Validation: Check competitor NPS, social media engagement, teacher forums

### D2C Commerce Specialist
- Primary: Interview target customer segment (age, income, shopping behavior)
- Secondary: SimilarWeb on competitors, category data from Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop
- Validation: Run $200 Facebook/TikTok ad test to measure CPC and conversion

### SaaS Growth Advisor
- Primary: Interview operations/IT/finance buyers (whoever signs the contract)
- Secondary: G2 reviews of competitors, LinkedIn job postings for "pain signal"
- Validation: Cold email 50 prospects and measure reply rate on problem statement

### F&B Operations Expert
- Primary: Visit competitor locations (mystery shopping), interview regular customers
- Secondary: Grab/Gojek/Baemin restaurant ranking data, local food blogger coverage
- Validation: Pop-up or farmers market test before full launch
