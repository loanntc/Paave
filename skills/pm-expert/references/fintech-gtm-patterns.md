# Fintech GTM Patterns — Vietnam / SEA

Reference for PM Expert skill. Load this file when working on GTM strategy in Vietnam or SEA
fintech markets.

---

## Vietnam Fintech Market Context

### Distribution Reality
- **Banking penetration:** ~70% but most users are savings-only; investment behavior is nascent
- **Mobile-first:** 90%+ smartphone penetration in urban; GTM must be mobile-native
- **Trust anchor:** State-owned banks (Vietcombank, BIDV, VietinBank) set the trust baseline
  that fintechs must overcome or partner with
- **Regulatory gatekeeper:** SSC (State Securities Commission) + SBV (State Bank of Vietnam)
  control what's possible; license timeline is a GTM variable, not a constant

### Common GTM Entry Points (with trade-offs)

| Entry Point | Pros | Cons | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank partnership | Trust, distribution, AUM | Slow, loss of control, rev share | VPS + VPBank |
| Employer/B2B2C | Captive audience, high intent | Long sales cycle, SME churn | Several payroll fintechs |
| Influencer/KOL | Fast awareness, low cost | Trust erosion risk, compliance exposure | Multiple crypto plays |
| Super-app embed | Scale fast | Dependency, feature constraints | MoMo ecosystem |
| Direct/performance | Full control | High CAC, trust barrier | KBSV, TCBS early |

### GTM Anti-Patterns in Vietnam Fintech

1. **"Educate the market first"** — usually means product-market fit isn't there yet. Real PMF
   doesn't require education campaigns.
2. **Copying Korean/US playbook directly** — regulatory, behavioral, and infrastructure context
   differs significantly. Localize the strategy, not just the UI.
3. **Launching to everyone** — diffuse GTM in fintech = zero trust. Start with a beachhead
   segment where you can build social proof fast.
4. **Ignoring compliance until post-launch** — SSC/SBV surprises kill timelines. Compliance
   is a GTM dependency.
5. **Pricing for growth before unit economics work** — zero-fee race to bottom erodes the
   category. Define your sustainable take rate early.

## SEA Expansion Considerations

When a Vietnam fintech considers SEA expansion:
- **Each market = new regulatory license** — no passporting like EU
- **Thailand, Indonesia:** Large markets but strong incumbents and complex local compliance
- **Singapore:** Smaller market but MAS license = regional credibility signal
- **Common failure mode:** Expanding before Vietnam unit economics are proven

## Securities-Specific GTM Notes

### Retail Investor Acquisition
- First trade is the hardest — reduce friction to zero for the first buy
- Social proof works: leaderboards, community features, copy-trading lower barrier
- Education content drives acquisition but rarely converts without a trigger event
  (market volatility, IPO buzz, bonus season)

### Trust Architecture for New Brokers
Sequence matters:
1. Regulatory license visibility (SSC logo, license number prominent)
2. Execution quality proof (order speed, fill rate — make it visible)
3. Social proof (user count, AUM, press)
4. Feature differentiation (last, not first)

Most new brokers invert this and lead with features. This is wrong.

### Pricing in Vietnamese Securities
- Standard brokerage fee: 0.15–0.25% per transaction
- Fee compression happening: some players at 0.1% or promo-zero
- Sustainable differentiation: not on fee, but on execution quality + insight + UX
- Zero-fee is a CAC tool, not a business model in this market
