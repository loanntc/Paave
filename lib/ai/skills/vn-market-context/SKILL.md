# Vietnam Stock Market Context

## Exchanges

| Exchange | Full Name | Typical stocks |
|---|---|---|
| HOSE | Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange | Large-cap (VIC, VHM, VNM, HPG, FPT, MWG, BID, VCB, VHM) |
| HNX | Hanoi Stock Exchange | Mid-cap + bonds |
| UPCOM | Unlisted Public Companies | Pre-listing, smaller cap |

## Trading Sessions (HOSE)

| Session | Time (ICT, UTC+7) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ATO | 09:00–09:15 | Opening call auction — price discovery |
| CONT | 09:15–11:30 | Continuous trading (morning) |
| Break | 11:30–13:00 | Lunch break |
| CONT | 13:00–14:30 | Continuous trading (afternoon) |
| ATC | 14:30–14:45 | Closing call auction |
| PUT-THROUGH | 14:45–15:00 | Block deal negotiation |
| CLOSED | After 15:00 | Market closed |

HNX follows a similar but slightly different schedule.

## Price Limits (Circuit Breakers)

- **HOSE stocks:** ±7% from the reference price per session
- **HNX stocks:** ±10% from reference
- **UPCOM stocks:** ±15% from reference
- **ETFs / CWs:** ±15%
- A stock hitting the ceiling (trần) or floor (sàn) triggers a trading halt for that stock

## Key Indices

- **VN-Index:** Broad market cap-weighted index of all HOSE stocks (~500 stocks)
- **VN30:** Top 30 largest HOSE stocks by cap + liquidity — the benchmark most institutions track
- **HNX-Index:** All HNX stocks
- **HNX30:** Top 30 HNX stocks
- **VN100:** Top 100 HOSE stocks

## Foreign Ownership Limits (FOL)

- Most stocks: 49% foreign ownership cap
- Banking sector: 30% cap (State Bank of Vietnam regulation)
- State-owned enterprises: varies, often 49%
- When a stock hits its FOL, foreigners can only sell — "room hết" (no foreign room left)
- Foreign buy/sell volumes are disclosed in real-time on HOSE

## T+2 Settlement

Vietnam uses T+2 settlement (trade day + 2 business days).
Shares bought today are available to sell on T+2.

## Reference Price

Each day's reference price = prior day's closing price.
Price limits are applied as ±% from the reference price, not the current price.

## Liquidity Characteristics

- Most liquid: VN30 constituents (VIC, VHM, VNM, FPT, HPG, etc.)
- Average session volume: 500M–1B shares across HOSE
- Foreign investors typically account for 15–25% of total value traded
- Retail investors dominate (~90% of accounts), making sentiment-driven moves common
